HOW WE DRY A STRUCTURE

01
Psychrometric baseline
Temperature, humidity, and grains-per-pound recorded inside, outside, and in the affected air.
02
Equipment sized to the load
Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers calculated per S500 — not guessed by room count.
03
Targeted cavity drying
Injectidry mats and wall systems dry hardwood and cavities without demolition where possible.
04
Daily monitoring to sign-off
Same techs, same meters, twice-daily readings until materials match dry standard — then we pull equipment.
